#### What is it?

Astronomers must have a flashlight with red light because it does not destroy dark adaptation of the human eyes.

#### Why did you make it?

The original intended use of this useful device was for the astronomical observing nights at the summer school of astronomy at Upice observatory.

#### What makes it special?

  * Repairable design with 3D printed chassis
  * Do not need batteries
  * Could be charged by standard smart-phone charger or by a computer USB port in few seconds
  * It is robustness and almost waterproof
